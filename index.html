Which bottle size should I choose?＋

Try the small bottle for occasional use, medium for an everyday kitchen companion, or large for sharing. The 250 g, 500 g and 1 kg options in this preview are proposed sizes, awaiting brand confirmation.

How should I store my honey?＋

Keep the lid closed and store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Always follow the storage guidance on your bottle.

Can I place a real order here?＋

This version is a demonstration. You can explore products and complete a simulated payment. No money is collected and no order is sent to Sweet Bridge.

How does delivery work?＋

The demo offers standard delivery at ₦1,500, express delivery at ₦3,000, or free pickup. Actual service areas, fees, pickup address and delivery times need confirmation before launch.

Can I return an order?＋

Sweet Bridge’s official returns and refund policy will be added before live ordering begins. No purchase is made through this preview.
